\subsection{Conception of Methodological Strategy}
The experimental framework was formulated through a systematic review of representation post-processing literature spanning 2018 to 2026. Rather than treating individual methods in isolation, we identified underlying mathematical connections across coordinate-level standardization \citep{timkey2021all}, subspace projection deflation \citep{mu2018all,ren2025correcting}, and spectral covariance regularizations \citep{su2021whitening,diera2024isotropy,li2026spectral}. 

Our architectural strategy was designed around four decoupled, idempotent pipeline stages (Download, Encode, Transform, Evaluate, Generate Tables) to ensure that neural network forward passes occur exactly once per model-task configuration, with downstream transformations and ranking evaluations executing in memory in milliseconds.

\subsection{Task Distribution and Operational Workflow}
Work was distributed among team members across four functional domains:
\begin{itemize}
\item Representation Extraction and Pipeline Infrastructure: Implemented Stage 0 and Stage 1 pipelines, Hugging Face Hub cache isolation, batch forward passes with MPS acceleration, and attention mask pooling operators with zero-token index clamping.
\item Mathematical Transformations and Numerical Algebra: Implemented Stage 2 linear post-processing algorithms, CPU-based positive semi-definite eigendecomposition, R2 orthogonal projection deflation, Soft-ZCA regularization, and Kneedle SNR knee detection for SpecTemp.
\item Benchmark Evaluation and Metric Auditing: Implemented Stage 3 evaluation runners, pytrec-eval C-bindings for nDCG@10, Recall@100, and MRR@10, scipy statistical correlation engines, and intrinsic space geometry diagnostics.
\item Research Table Synthesis and Manuscript Authoring: Developed the Polars LazyFrame table compilation engine generating all 49 publication-grade tables, conducted cross-paradigm statistical analysis, and authored the technical manuscript.
\end{itemize}
