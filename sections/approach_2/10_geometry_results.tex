\section{Intrinsic Space Geometry Diagnostics}
\label{sec:geometry_results}

Intrinsic space geometry diagnostics quantify representation space distributions across five metrics: centroid norm ($\|\mu\|_2$), average pairwise cosine similarity, Maximum Explainable Variance of the leading eigenvalue (MEV Top-1), Normalized Intrinsic Dimensionality (NID), and global isotropy (IsoScore).

\subsection{ArguAna Intrinsic Geometry}
Table~\ref{tab:arguana_geometry} and Table~\ref{tab:arguana_geometry_comp} report geometric diagnostics on ArguAna under full-dimension and compressed representations ($k=128$).

\input{sections/approach_2/tables/geometry/arguana_geometry.tex}

\paragraph{Analysis of ArguAna Geometry}
\begin{itemize}
\item Baseline Tier Disparity: Base models exhibit severe origin shift (centroid norm 0.8992 on mean pooling; 0.8123 on last token) and narrow representation cones (average cosine 0.8070 and 0.6583), yielding low isotropy (IsoScore 0.0108 to 0.0309). Dedicated models maintain balanced dispersion (centroid norm ~0.46; average cosine ~0.21; IsoScore 0.1135 to 0.1679).
\item Last Token Rogue Dimension: MEV top-1 reaches 0.2632 on Qwen3.5-Base under last token pooling, indicating that over 26\% of all representation energy concentrates along a single coordinate axis.
\item Error Cancellation and Whitening: R2 reduces centroid norm from 0.8992 to 0.0434, while Soft-ZCA flattens the spectrum (MEV drops to 0.0045; IsoScore jumps to 0.4733 on base mean pooling and 0.8573 on dedicated models).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Analysis of FiQA2018 Geometry}
\begin{itemize}
\item Acute Anisotropy: Base models exhibit extreme cosine crowding (average cosine 0.7952 to 0.8267) and high centroid norms (0.8911 to 0.9070). Last token pooling produces MEV top-1 of 0.2363 on Qwen3.5-Base.
\item Progressive Component Purging: ABTT-1 through ABTT-3 cut MEV on Gemma-3-Base from 0.1223 down to 0.0602, raising IsoScore from 0.0239 to 0.0564. Soft-ZCA reduces MEV to 0.0054 and raises IsoScore to 0.4962.
\item Compressed Subspaces ($k=128$): Whitening at $k=128$ drives IsoScore to 0.9453, while unrotated prefix slicing retains severe origin offset (centroid norm 0.6811; average cosine 0.4657).
\end{itemize}
