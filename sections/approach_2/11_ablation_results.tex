\section{Token Pooling Ablations}
\label{sec:ablation_results}

Token pooling ablations evaluate the performance disparity between sequence mean pooling (averaging hidden states across active attention tokens) and final token pooling (hidden state of the final non-padding token) on un-fine-tuned base causal language models (\texttt{google/gemma-3-1b-pt} and \texttt{Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B-Base}). Table~\ref{tab:pooling_penalty_ir} reports retrieval performance across FiQA2018, ArguAna, and SCIDOCS (nDCG@10), while Table~\ref{tab:pooling_penalty_sts} reports semantic similarity across SICK-R, STSBenchmark, and STS22.v2 (Spearman $\rho$). Both tables are in Appendix~\ref{app:app2}.

\paragraph{On retrieval, last-token pooling loses everywhere.}
For both models on all three IR datasets, last-token nDCG@10 is far below mean pooling. Averaged over the two models, last-token pooling keeps about a third of the mean-pooled score on ArguAna, about a quarter on FiQA2018, and about a sixth on SCIDOCS. The models differ most on FiQA2018, where Gemma-3 keeps about two fifths of its mean-pooled score and Qwen3.5 almost none.

\paragraph{Post-processing widens the retrieval gap.}
Every method except \texttt{mc} leaves last-token pooling below mean pooling for both models on every IR dataset. Soft-ZCA raises both pooling modes, but mean pooling gains more, so the absolute gap after Soft-ZCA is larger than before on all three datasets.

\paragraph{On similarity, the sign depends on the dataset and the model.}
On raw SICK-R embeddings, last-token pooling scores above mean pooling for both models, clearly for Gemma-3 and marginally for Qwen3.5. On STSBenchmark the models disagree: last token is ahead for Gemma-3 and behind for Qwen3.5. On STS22.v2, last token falls more than 40 points below mean pooling for both models.

\paragraph{Post-processing moves the similarity gap in different directions.}
On STSBenchmark, Soft-ZCA puts last-token pooling at or above mean pooling for both models. On SICK-R the opposite happens: after Soft-ZCA or any ABTT variant, last token falls below mean pooling for both models. On STS22.v2, every method except the random control narrows the gap. Soft-ZCA and ABTT-3 narrow it by about ten points, but it stays above 30 points. \texttt{mc} narrows it further, but only because it lowers mean pooling.
